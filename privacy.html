Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who uses this app

Hermes Secretary is a personal tool used only by its owner, Thadchai Suksaran. It is not available to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app may access the owner's Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Gmail and contacts, limited to the permissions the owner approves on the consent screen. It uses this data only to manage the owner's tasks, schedule, habits and records at the owner's request.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer.
  • To answer requests, relevant text may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen (currently OpenCode). It is not sent anywhere else.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train general AI models by this app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
